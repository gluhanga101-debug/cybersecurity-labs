# Cybersecurity Labs

Hands-on security labs completed alongside graduate coursework in Information Systems Security at the University of Denver and independent study. Each lab covers a specific area of offensive or defensive security, documented with methodology, evidence, and findings.

## About Me

I'm a graduate student completing an MSc in Information Systems Security at the University of Denver, currently working as an infosec intern at the DU SOC where I use tools including Stellar Cyber, Palo Alto, CrowdStrike Falcon, Proofpoint, and Rapid7 InsightVM day to day. My background is in business and economics, and I approach security from both a technical and analytical perspective.
